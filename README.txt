ROCKY AND LUNA’S MATH JOURNEY

Open START-HERE.html or index.html. Keep this entire folder together.

Excite: Grade 2. Inspire: Grade 3. Six levels: A, B, C, AA, BB, CC.
480 source activities across the two grades, with picture matching, cloze and picture questions mixed through the levels. Listening and Excel assessment are included.

All activity scripts, styles and required illustrations are inside this folder. No installation is needed. Listening uses an English voice available on the device. Browser progress stays local to that browser and file location.

GRADE ENTRY AND CHARACTERS
Rocky is the brown striped tabby for Excite Grade 2. Luna is the black cat for Inspire Grade 3. Both are based on the supplied reference and appear beside their grade buttons. Selecting a grade opens a confirmation popup with its cat. Stay here and Escape leave the current grade and activity in place.

The activity header uses two compact rows on laptops, with more room for illustrations and answer choices. Narrower screens use the Question/Picture switch.

Artwork was made with the built-in image_gen tool. Native PNGs, transparent WebP copies, the supplied reference, and generation prompts are in assets/.

VOICE NAVIGATION FIX
Each reading is tied to the current question. Next, Back, grade, level, topic and shuffle changes cancel active and queued reading before changing the question. Picture matching reads the current sentence and picture letters, without hidden descriptions of other scenes. Stop also cancels a reading that has not started yet. Switching views or leaving the tab stops speech.

VOICE SPEED
The Speed selector stays beside Listen and Stop on every activity page, including tablets and narrow screens. Choose Very slow, Slow, Steady or Fast. A speed change during reading restarts the current question at the selected speed. The choice stays saved as you change pages, levels, topics and grades, and when you return to this browser.
